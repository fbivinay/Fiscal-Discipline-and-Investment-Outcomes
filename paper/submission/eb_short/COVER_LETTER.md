Dear Editors,

Please consider the attached manuscript, "Fiscal Discipline and Foreign Direct
Investment across Indian States: A Ten-State Panel Cannot Separate Debt from
Region", for publication in the Economics Bulletin under the **Preliminary
Results** category.

The note asks whether the fiscal discipline of Indian states predicts the foreign
direct investment they receive. Using a balanced panel of the ten largest
FDI-recipient states over FY 2019-20 to FY 2023-24, built from audited state
finance accounts in the Sixteenth Finance Commission's annexures, we find that
states carrying persistently lower public debt do receive more FDI. Five
estimators drawn from three families — pooled and fixed-effects panel regression,
a Mundlak decomposition, rank correlation, LASSO, and SHAP importance from a
random forest — recover the association with the same sign, and a Mundlak
specification rejects equality of the between-state and within-state coefficients
at p = 0.017.

We submit it as Preliminary Results because the central contribution is a
negative one. Seventy-two per cent of the cross-state variance in debt lies
between four regional groups, and adding regional indicators reverses the pooled
coefficient's sign. Ten states across four regions cannot separate a fiscal
signal from a regional one, and we report that identification problem as the
result rather than as a caveat. The note also specifies the design that would
resolve it — a twenty-six state cross-section whose fiscal side is already
published in the same Finance Commission annexures — and reports a preliminary
check on that wider sample.

Two disclosures, in keeping with your General Publication Terms:

First, a longer working-paper version of this research, from which this note is
drawn, is publicly available in the project's open repository at
https://github.com/fbivinay/Fiscal-Discipline-and-Investment-Outcomes, together
with the complete dataset and analysis code. It has not been submitted to or
published in any journal. The manuscript cites the repository in its data
availability statement.

Second, the manuscript is not under consideration at any other journal, the work
has not been published elsewhere, and both authors and our institution approve of
this submission.

We have also uploaded a ZIP archive containing the analysis code and the data
panel, so that every estimate in the note can be reproduced from the audited
source series.

Thank you for your consideration.
